Privacy

Textforge runs entirely in your browser. Text, JSON, files and patterns you paste or open are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or stored on any server.

What's stored

Your browser's local storage keeps only two small values: the last tool you used (so the home page can offer to take you back) and your cookie choice. You can clear them anytime by clearing site data for this site.

Advertising

Textforge is free because it shows ads. Ad partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. If you choose "Essential only", only non-personalised ads are shown. Ads never have access to the text you enter in the tools. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

Hosting logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability. These logs never contain anything you type into the tools.
