How to use the URL encoder


  1. Choose Encode or Decode and paste your text or URL on the left.
  2. Pick Component to encode a single query value (it escapes &, =, / and ?), or Full URL to keep the structure of a complete link intact.
  3. When decoding form data or search queries, tick + means space so plus signs turn into spaces.
  4. Paste a complete link starting with https:// to see the URL breakdown: protocol, host, path, fragment and each query parameter, already decoded.
  5. The HTML entities tab converts < > & and quotes to safe entities and back.


Frequently asked questions


What is URL encoding?
URL encoding, or percent-encoding, replaces characters that are not allowed in URLs with a % followed by two hex digits. A space becomes %20 and é becomes %C3%A9, because non-ASCII characters are first converted to UTF-8 bytes.
Should I use component or full URL encoding?
Use Component for anything you put inside a URL, such as a search term or a redirect address in a query parameter. Use Full URL only for a whole address you want to make valid while keeping its :, /, ? and & characters working.
Why does decoding fail with “Malformed % sequence”?
A % must be followed by two hex digits that form valid UTF-8. A stray % sign, or a link cut off in the middle of a character, can’t be decoded. Encode the literal % as %25 instead.
Why do I see + instead of spaces?
HTML forms and many search engines encode spaces as +. Tick “+ means space” when decoding those. In other parts of a URL a + is a real plus sign, so leave it off.
